## Recording Tips for Video Capture
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record at 1920x1080 (16:9).
2. **Audio:** Use a clean, noise-cancelled microphone.
3. **Pacing:** Follow the 30-second blocks closely so the video does not exceed 3 minutes.
4. **Visual Cues:** Use gentle cursor movements and highlight interactive pills when clicked.
