During severe winter smog episodes across Indian metros, millions of families check AQI numbers daily. But an AQI reading of 425 tells you only that the air is toxic — not how to adapt your family’s morning routine, when to flush indoor air safely, or how to commute without multiplying deep-lung particulate exposure.

Existing apps stop at displaying alarms. We built an evidence-based, grounded environmental intelligence platform that bridges the gap between atmospheric science and daily urban living.

• Powered by Google Gemini with server-side API key protection and multi-layer prompt defense.
