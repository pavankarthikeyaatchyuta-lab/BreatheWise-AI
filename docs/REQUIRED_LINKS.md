## 2. Regulatory & Data Source References Cited in Application

1. **Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) NAQI Methodology (2014):**
   - National Air Quality Index (NAQI) Breakpoint and Sub-Index Equations.
   - 8 criteria pollutants: $PM_{2.5}, PM_{10}, NO_2, SO_2, CO, O_3, NH_3, Pb$.
   - URL: `https://cpcb.nic.in/national-air-quality-index/`

2. **Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) Statutory GRAP:**
   - Statutory Direction under Section 12 of the CAQM Act, 2021 for NCR and Adjoining Areas.
   - Stages I–IV triggers: Stage I (>200 Poor), Stage II (>300 Very Poor), Stage III (>400 Severe), Stage IV (>450 Severe+).
   - URL: `https://caqm.nic.in/`

3. **Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Baseline Database:**
   - CEA Baseline Carbon Dioxide Emissions Database Version 22.0 (August 2026, FY 2025–26): $0.675\text{ tCO}_2/\text{MWh}$ ($0.675\text{ kg CO}_2/\text{kWh}$ Weighted Average including renewable generation and captive power injection, adjusted for cross-border electricity transfers).
   - Historical benchmarks: Version 19.0 (FY 2022–23, $0.716\text{ kg CO}_2/\text{kWh}$) and Annual Report (FY 2023–24, $0.727\text{ tCO}_2/\text{MWh}$).
   - URL: `https://cea.nic.in/`

4. **Automotive Research Association of India (ARAI) Emission Factors:**
   - Comprehensive Emission Factors for On-road Transport (Draft Guidelines).
   - Petrol Car ($0.03\text{ g PM}_{2.5}/\text{pkm}, 144\text{ g CO}_2/\text{pkm}$).
   - URL: `https://www.araiindia.com/`

5. **Petroleum Conservation Research Association (PCRA, India):**
   - Anti-Idling Fuel Conservation and Exhaust Mitigation Protocol.
   - Switching off ignition at signals $>15-20$ seconds.
   - URL: `https://pcra.org/`

6. **OpenAQ v3 API:**
   - URL: `https://openaq.org/` (Licensed under CC-BY-4.0 / ODC-BY).

7. **Open-Meteo Air Quality Model (ECMWF CAMS / SILAM):**
   - URL: `https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api` (Licensed under CC-BY-4.0).
